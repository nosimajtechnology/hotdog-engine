# Storyboards and continuity

Use latest approved scene and rendering contract. A board is a shot plan, not a
turnaround. Default SCENE to 4-6 connected shots; about 4-5 main beats suits 15s
H3 when useful. Explicit six-shot requests remain six. Match layout and prompt count.

Give each shot purpose, framing, pose, readable action, contact/ownership,
spatial anchor and end-state. Hook immediately, show cause/effect, escalate and
pay off visibly. Vary motivated in-engine coverage. Keep screen direction and
camera side; establish transitions before crossing/changing locations. Timing
must cover duration without gaps/overlap; exact cuts express intent.

## Internal ledger

```text
PROJECT: mode | route | duration | aspect
HOTDOG: asset/version | face | bun/sausage/mustard | limbs | pose | outfit
SECONDARIES: separate identity | position | outfit | condition
WORLD: location | time | weather | lighting | permanent changes
PROPS: owner | position | orientation | condition
GEOGRAPHY: camera side | screen direction | entrances | landmarks
ACTION: completed | current | unresolved | next event
BUILD: profile/version | source of selection | rendering contract
GROUNDING: inspected sources | assigned roles | limitations
REFERENCES: ordered inputs and individual roles
MOTION: profile | camera purpose | contact points
STATE CHANGE: pre-state | change-only delta | post-state
AUTHORITY: approved frame | approved board | approved final state
MODEL: provider/host | endpoint | fields | exact prompt limit
AUDIO: music | ambience/effects | dialogue/narration route
REPAIRS: failure | protected layers | attempted correction | outcome
```

Update at approvals/state changes. Keep internal unless requested. Retain known
model/audio/route; “continue” preserves them. Reset only for a new project or
explicit change. Resolve missing prior state rather than inventing approval.

## Crossovers

Each character gets independent identity reference/text lock, anatomy, outfit,
scale, position and condition. Retrieve provided/available secondary authority;
ask for missing required reference only when necessary. Hotdog stays first in
H3 R2V. Secondaries never inherit bun/mustard/face/limbs; Hotdog never inherits
their equipment. No dependency on another engine's installation.

Establish ownership before/after handoffs. Use reachable surfaces and pause at
contact for short paws. Identical objects still need separate IDs/owners. Avoid
changing hands and camera side in one ambiguous beat.

## Episode carryover

Advance each board from prior approved end-state. A delivered parcel stays
delivered until an explicit new action changes that; do not return it to Hotdog's
paws. Preserve consumed items, damage, clothing, time and world changes. Do not
replay setup or regenerate prior boards. A fifth board must earn a new beat.
