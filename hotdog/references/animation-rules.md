# Model-neutral animation direction

Keep identity, rendering, format, route, motion and provider separate. Build a
brief: premise/hook, duration/aspect, starting authority, character locks, spatial
anchors, ordered beats, camera purpose, contact, end-state, audio. Then use
[model-adapters.md](model-adapters.md).

- Place handles, parcels and buttons within short-paw reach; use two paws for
  larger objects. Show stable contact before lifting/releasing.
- Use short steps, torso tilt, head turns and readable poses. Preserve bun depth
  and attachment positions; no elastic arms or human hands.
- Keep two arms/two legs through stairs, turns and occlusion. No added feet or
  quadrupedal transitions. Front face/mustard remain separate from plain rear.
- Do not pack running, spinning, grabbing, speaking and transfer into one beat.
  Use motivated cuts, close-ups or occlusion when they clarify fragile contact.

Preserve seed premise: immediate hook, cause/effect, escalation, payoff. Around
4-5 principal beats fit 15s when useful, but honor explicit counts. Use contiguous
timing. Do not promise exact cuts/speech, render speed or demonstrated performance.

## Rotation / bumper

Default six seconds, one take, one primary motion, fixed camera, no text/dialogue.
Distinguish character turntable from camera orbit. For “one full turn,” specify
one direction through 360 degrees with no stops, snaps, reversal or second turn.
Return naturally to the first pose for a loop. Avoid angle checkpoints that
cause stopping. No contact sheet required.

## Audio

Default no music; ambience/effects allowed; no unsolicited dialogue. “NO AUDIO”
means silence. Persist choices on continuation. An approved narration plan can
override dialogue defaults. Prefer separate voiceover for exact ad narration
and omit its spoken script from the video prompt to avoid duplicate voices.
Use prose audio directions unless a verified host offers controls. End cards
and text are optional.
