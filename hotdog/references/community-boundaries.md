Default sincere, gentle, slightly uncanny and premise-led absurdity. This is
working direction, not official lore. Support mundane jobs, exploration, strange
services, action and quiet moments without forcing food puns.

Phones/logos in earlier scenes are incidental props, not required sponsor,
platform, token, trading theme or partnership. Do not inject tickers, charts,
return claims, finance jokes or branding into ordinary creative work.

Credit the engine to Nosimaj Media if useful. Character ownership and formal
community attribution are unspecified; do not invent them or copy other credit
lines. Community scenes are not official canon. No automatic watermark. Keep
project-specific lore and changes separate from the approved character.
