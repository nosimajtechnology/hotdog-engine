# Hotdog identity and construction

Use [hotdog-character-sheet-v1.png](../assets/hotdog-character-sheet-v1.png)
unchanged. It is the approved six-view identity authority, not an extracted
game asset. 1536 x 1024, RGB, white background, no alpha.
SHA-256: `f85cf3e0b1ad946344a741ad3efc822ef286c78f9f9a4935392b37036567444e`.

| Part | Preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Small upright bipedal canine integrated into a vertical hot dog; compact proportions |
| Face | Tan forehead, broad cream cheeks/muzzle, dark faceted nose, muted gray-violet eyes, gentle closed mouth |
| Head | Upper front between bun halves; bounded by bun silhouette |
| Bun | Two long golden-brown halves, pale inner edges, stable depth/spacing/length and angular planes |
| Sausage | Dull brick-red center, rounded exposed tip above forehead |
| Mustard | One yellow sinuous front stripe below face, fixed placement/scale |
| Back | Plain red sausage between golden bun backs; no rear face or mustard |
| Ears | Small tan triangular tips behind upper bun; viewpoint and occlusion determine visibility |
| Arms | Exactly two short tan arms, pale paw tips, same attachment positions as sheet |
| Legs | Exactly two short tan legs, chunky pale paw feet and simple toe marks |
| Lower rear | Tiny pale nub as shown; never enlarge to a curled tail |
| Surface | Low-resolution painted detail, modest angular mesh, simple game shading |

Bun and sausage are persistent identity geometry. Head and limbs articulate
around them; sitting, walking and turning cannot swap, duplicate, flatten,
dissolve or detach them. Do not invent straps, zipper, removable hood, concealed
human body, human fingers or long elastic limbs. Do not substitute generic Doge,
dachshund, quadruped in costume, human mascot or modern toy render.

The six views are rotations of one character. In ordinary scenes prohibit sheet
layout, white backdrop, multiple copies and unwanted text. A profile may occlude
the farther arm/leg; do not draw hidden limbs through the body. Rear views must
not mirror face or mustard onto the back.

## Variation and studies

Vary pose, expression, activity, props, camera, lighting and world. Expression
studies preserve eye size/spacing, muzzle volume and head/body proportion.
Neutral canon does not make all scenes motionless or expressionless. Outfit
additions preserve silhouette and attachments. Bun removal, condiment changes,
new anatomy or costume replacement require explicit creative instruction;
record the project delta without overwriting the base.

For an unchanged canonical turnaround, reuse the existing approved sheet when
it satisfies the request. A newly requested study becomes canonical only after
approval. “No scenery” does not imply alpha. If transparency is requested, use
an image edit and inspect the actual alpha channel. White or checkerboard is
not transparency. Never alter the source merely to package it.

## Standalone T2V identity

Adapt this full block into every text-only video prompt; never refer to an unseen sheet:

```text
One small upright bipedal canine integrated into a vertical hot dog. Compact
body with two elongated golden-brown bun halves and pale inner edges flanking
a dull brick-red sausage; its rounded tip rises above his forehead. His tan
face emerges from the upper front between the buns, with broad cream cheeks
and muzzle, dark faceted nose, muted gray-violet eyes and gentle closed mouth.
Head stays inside the bun silhouette. Small tan triangular ear tips sit behind
the upper bun. One yellow sinuous mustard stripe runs down the front below
his face. Exactly two short tan arms with pale paw tips and two short tan legs
with chunky pale paw feet and simple toe marks. Rear: plain red sausage between
golden bun backs, tiny pale lower nub, no rear face or mustard. Keep bun depth
and attachments stable through motion. Painted low-resolution textures, mildly
angular mesh planes, simple original PS2 game shading.
```

T2V produces interpretations, not a new canonical face. Recommend Character
Lock for identity/anatomy drift, Classic Control for exact composition/geography.
