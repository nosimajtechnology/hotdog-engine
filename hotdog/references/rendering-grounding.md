# Game grounding

Every new game build triggers grounding, including inferred/default PS2,
prompt-only game concepts and blank studies. Reuse approved rendering contracts
for continuation/repair. Refresh only for a changed build or fidelity failure.

1. Resolve platform/title from instruction, project state, premise, then original
   2003-2004 PS2 default.
2. Retrieve and visually inspect about three to five useful original-platform
   gameplay/in-engine screenshots when available. Search text is not visual
   inspection. Blank studies need mesh/material evidence, not scenery.
3. Cover relevant mesh, diffuse texture, density, lighting and camera. Choose one
   dominant lineage and at most two close comparables for narrow roles.
4. Record URLs, platform/provenance, observed traits, assigned roles and gaps.
   Derive a compact contract before generation.
5. Supply references only for assigned roles; generate and check identity/fidelity.
   Grounding screenshots normally remain internal research inputs.

Exclude remasters, changed ports, texture packs, enhanced emulation, fan renders,
promotional CGI and AI images as historical evidence. Credibly identified
extracted models can supplement screenshots. Do not redistribute third-party
screenshots in the public skill package.

Start canine mesh/diffuse research with Dog's Life on original PS2. The PRD's
[period screenshot source](https://games.kikizo.com/reviews/ps2/dogslife.asp)
is a research seed, not proof of fresh inspection. Its dog identity never
overrides Hotdog. Choose world title from premise; explicit GTA San Andreas
requires original PS2 GTA San Andreas evidence. Do not force every environment
into Dog's Life or average unrelated games.

```text
BUILD: profile/version, platform/title, selection reason
EVIDENCE: source URL, platform proof, observed feature, narrow role
MESH: face/bun/paw planes, geometry density and silhouettes
TEXTURE: diffuse detail, filtering, repetition/material construction
LIGHT: vertex/baked behavior, shadows, broad highlight restraint
WORLD: modular density, skybox/foliage/draw distance, spatial anchors
CAMERA: in-engine crop, motivated movement/coverage
EXCLUDE: observed-era contradictions
LIMITATIONS: uncertain provenance, missing views, unavailable research
```

Use readable mesh planes, small diffuse maps, painted facial detail, restrained
vertex/baked light, broad highlights and period shadows. Match modular assets,
foliage, skybox, draw distance and density to the chosen game. Avoid modern fur,
bread microdetail, subdivision, PBR, cinematic depth of field and modern light.
Do not default PS2 to PS1 affine warping, vertex snapping or severe dithering.
Grain/blur/pixelation cannot make modern assets period-correct.

If trustworthy search/visual retrieval is unavailable, state “Using a historical-
profile fallback; fresh game screenshots could not be inspected.” Continue with
a useful provisional contract. Never invent sources or claim unseen inspection.
