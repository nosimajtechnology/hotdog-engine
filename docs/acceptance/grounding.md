# Visual acceptance grounding, 2026-09-05

Inspected three full-resolution 640x480 original-PS2 Dog's Life screenshots from the contemporary [Kikizo review](https://games.kikizo.com/reviews/ps2/dogslife.asp), dated November 4, 2003 and labeled PS2. The media identifiers are 03.jpg (canine profile/waterfall), 05.jpg (street rear view) and 06.jpg (cafe terrace).

Observed: modest angular canine meshes, painted diffuse facial/body detail, repeated terrain/pavement textures, modular fences/buildings, low-density trees/foliage, broad lighting and simple shadows. The waterfall image has strong sun rays; this scene-specific effect was not made a Hotdog default. These images govern rendering/environment construction only. Hotdog's approved sheet governs character identity. Third-party screenshot bytes are not distributed.

Contract: original PS2 diffuse-painted mesh construction, simple courtyard assets, low work surface reachable by short paws, white plaster wall/blue door spatial anchors, no HUD, no modern material microdetail or photographic depth of field. Visual test sequence: parcel on platform -> lifted -> carried -> delivered at door, paws empty. No new canonical character sheet was created.
